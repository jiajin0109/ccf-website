FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does a lower price mean lower quality?

Savings come from our development process. AI handles repetitive coding tasks, while we focus on requirements, testing and quality checks before delivery.

How can you deliver quickly?

We reuse proven components from our existing products and use AI-assisted development to get a working version ready sooner.

Can I request changes after launch?

Ongoing adjustments are available through a maintenance plan. Contact us on LINE for minor changes; larger features are quoted for your approval first.

Where is the data stored?

Cloud products use servers with encrypted connections, separate company data and regular backups. Custom projects can also run on your own server or cloud account.

Can I try a product first?

Yes. Paid products offer a free trial, and US Stock Opportunity Radar is free to use. For custom projects, we can show a demonstration after the initial discussion.
