01Manufacturing and equipment integration

Factory Pre-production Management System

Preparation checklists integrated with motion stages, inkjet printers and barcode scanners

A chemical-materials factory needed to prepare multiple molds and checklists before production, alongside motion stages, inkjet printers and barcode scanners on the shop floor. We brought manual checks, equipment control and record keeping into one system.

Explore this project＋

Key features

  • Create pre-production checklists so operators can check molds, quantities and handling notes in order.
  • Compare the current checklist with the previous batch and highlight additions, removals and changes to reduce repeated checking.
  • Retrieve product information by scanning a barcode, reducing manual searches and data-entry errors.
  • Keep records of each production run so managers can trace issues and look up historical data.

Equipment and platform integration

  • Control the motion stage and inkjet printer together and adjust their timing to keep product positioning consistent.
  • Determine product orientation and print content automatically to reduce errors from repeated manual typing.
  • Report equipment status on screen so supervisors can monitor startup, positioning and faults.

Benefits

  • Reduce time spent explaining preparation checklists and repeating the same instructions.
  • Reduce manual entry and transcription errors, including incorrect print content and missed steps.
  • Support retries and recovery when equipment faults occur, helping operators resolve issues more efficiently.

03Restaurant chain operations

Central Warehouse and POS Integration for a Restaurant Chain

A platform for store replenishment, dispatch and receiving, POS, LINE loyalty and operations management

We helped a grilled-food restaurant chain plan an integrated headquarters platform. The project replaces handwritten store replenishment orders, photos sent through LINE and manual consolidation with online requests, central aggregation, dispatch, receiving and inventory records. The scope also plans a store POS interface, receipt-printer and cash-drawer integration, loyalty points, attendance scheduling and an owner dashboard, giving headquarters a central view of stores and a repeatable foundation for expansion.

Explore this project＋

Key features

  • Define accounts and role permissions for owners, headquarters, warehouse staff, store managers and employees.
  • Let stores submit replenishment orders online instead of sending photos of handwritten orders through LINE, reducing omissions, misreading and manual consolidation.
  • Aggregate store demand centrally by store and item to support preparation and dispatch.
  • Record central dispatch, store receipt confirmation and discrepancy reports, with a history of shipments, receipts and stock movements.
  • Plan a store POS interface for product selection, orders, additions, checkout, daily closing, printing and cash-drawer control.
  • Use a local-first POS design with SQLite storing orders and transactions during service and synchronizing to headquarters in the background.
  • Reserve extension modules for POS sales synchronization, loyalty points, coupons, attendance scheduling, opening and closing checklists, and store task assignments.
  • Provide an owner dashboard and reports covering replenishment, dispatch, receiving, stock, waste, sales and membership data.

Equipment and platform integration

  • The store POS client can be built with Electron and Node.js for local device control and offline storage.
  • Recommend thermal printers supporting ESC/POS commands for kitchen tickets, counter tickets and customer receipts.
  • Trigger electronic cash drawers through the thermal printer's drawer port and record the time, operator and reason for opening.
  • Synchronize products, orders, replenishment requests, receiving and daily closing between the cloud headquarters backend and store POS clients through REST APIs.
  • Integrate LINE Login, LIFF or LINE Messaging API as entry points for loyalty, attendance, notifications and marketing.
  • Evaluate vendor APIs, read-only database access, automated exports or a custom POS according to the capabilities offered by the POS provider.

Benefits

  • Systematize store replenishment to reduce handwriting, photos, missed LINE messages and manual consolidation.
  • Give the central warehouse visibility into store demand and improve preparation, dispatch and receiving follow-up.
  • Keep stock-movement records to trace issues and analyze shortages and waste.
  • Once POS and backend data are integrated, sales records can support reports, loyalty points, replenishment suggestions and operations decisions.
  • Support repeat visits and brand engagement through LINE loyalty points, coupons and messaging.
  • Reduce communication work for store managers and headquarters through attendance scheduling, opening and closing checklists, and store task assignments.
  • Adopt replenishment and warehouse workflows first, then extend the modular platform with POS, loyalty, attendance and replenishment planning.
